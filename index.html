Patterns

What the record says so far. Each panel stays blank until there is enough data to support it rather than showing a number it cannot stand behind.

The cluster, day by day

One row per channel, one column per day. Vertical stripes mean the channels are moving together; blank columns are clear days.

Does the cluster lead the headache?

Mean cluster score on the days around a headache onset, against your baseline. A rise on the left is a premonitory window; a tail on the right is a postdromal one. Same-day correlation would tell you almost nothing.

Do the five move as one?

Correlation between each pair of channels. If they co-vary as a unit, that is evidence of one underlying process rather than five separate complaints.

Is the fog the room, or the migraine?

Fog against the rest of the cluster, split by that day's CO₂. If only fog climbs with CO₂, that is the air — a mechanism worth separating out rather than logging as another bad day.

Pressure against the cluster

Days the barometer moved more than the daily threshold, against days it stayed put.

Your data

Access

The passcode is a gate, not encryption: this is a static page, so anyone determined enough can read its source. It keeps the dashboard from being readable by someone who simply lands on the URL. Your ratings never leave this browser except to the private channel above.
